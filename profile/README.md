Arcana Land provides open standards, tools and resources for exploring digital tarot decks and associated esoterica. Our goal is to make digital tarot accessible, open and free.

Explore our repositories:
- Specifications for tarot decks and esoterica.
- Tools for exploring tarot content and creating your own.
- Reference implementations showcasing best practices.

In the future: [https://arcana.land](http://arcana.land) will be an ad-free showcase of freely licensed and public domain Tarot decks and esoterica. For now, [Pocket Tarot](https://busterbenson.com/tarot) is a great alternative.
